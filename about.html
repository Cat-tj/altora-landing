The way of thinking

It's not forcing one product for everything.

Operation needs don't always start with new applications. Sometimes all it needs to be done is a workflow, data, device, access, or the way the team sees conditions on the field.

Altora starts with that context. From there, we determine whether the needs are more precise using products ready for integration, custom systems, AI vision, sensors, devices, or integration among them.

Operating model

From real conditions to usable systems.

One similar process was used when Altora accompanied software needs, AI, hardware, or integration.

  1. 01

    Understand the operation

    Reading processes, obstacles, people, data and devices involved before determining solutions.

  2. 02

    Specify approach

    Selecting the most relevant path, from a product ready to use to a more specific development.

  3. 03

    Implementation & escort

    Build, integrate, test, then help the team run the system gradually.
