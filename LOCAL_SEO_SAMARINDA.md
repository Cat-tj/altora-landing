Purpose: build topic authority through useful, distinct pages—not doorway pages or repetitive city-keyword copy.

## Pillar
- `/samarinda.html` — overview: how Altora approaches software, applications, websites, systems, AI, hardware, and integration in Samarinda.

4. `/software-house-samarinda.html`
   - Intent: selecting a software partner and mapping a technology approach.
   - Evidence: projects hub plus software / AI / hardware / integration capabilities.
   - Primary services: `/services.html`, `/portfolio.html`.

## Deferred until there is stronger verified source material
- city-specific cost/price pages
- industry-specific city pages
- SLA/support claims
- client/result pages without public evidence

## Internal linking
- Every spoke links to pillar, its primary service page, 2–3 directly relevant documented projects, and consultation.
- Pillar links to all spokes under a “Panduan kebutuhan di Samarinda” block.
- Service hub links to pillar and its relevant spokes.
- Sitemap contains all pages.

## Content rule
- One clear job per page; do not duplicate headings, FAQs, or paragraphs between spokes.
- Mention Samarinda only where it provides visitor context. Capability claims must map to existing services or documented projects.
