# Altora National SEO Architecture

## What ships now
- `/indonesia.html`: national intent hub for software, applications, websites, AI, IoT, hardware, and integration across Indonesia.
- Service pages: the canonical capability source.
- Portfolio documentation: evidence source.
- Samarinda cluster: a regional/local cluster supported by locally relevant context.

## What does NOT ship as city pages now
No pages for Jakarta, Bandung, Surabaya, Bali, Makassar, Medan, etc. until Altora has genuine city-specific operational context, project evidence, local partnership/office information, or original city-focused resource content.

Reason: multiplying identical pages with city names is doorway-page risk and weakens brand trust.

## National intent pathways
- Software: `/jasa-pembuatan-software-indonesia.html` (custom systems, workflow, dashboard, integration)
- Applications: `/jasa-pembuatan-aplikasi-indonesia.html` (web/mobile operational applications)
- Websites: `/jasa-pembuatan-website-indonesia.html` (company profile, CMS, SEO-ready information architecture)
- Partner selection: `/software-house-indonesia.html` (evaluation and discovery orientation)

Every page must have: distinct user job, evidence links, clear internal service link, consultation route, accurate metadata/canonical, and non-duplicated copy.
